# Code of Conduct

The glossary is a shared standards-adjacent vocabulary resource. Contributors are expected to collaborate professionally, ground disagreements in sources and evidence, and keep reviews focused on term quality, authority boundaries, and downstream impact.

Harassment, personal attacks, sustained disruption, or exclusionary behavior are not acceptable.

Maintainers may moderate comments, close threads, or restrict participation when behavior prevents constructive work.

Report concerns through the repository maintainers' normal private contact channel.
