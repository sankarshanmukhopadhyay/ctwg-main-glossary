# Project Lineage

The Trust Infrastructure Glossary originated as a fork of `trustoverip/ctwg-main-glossary`.

Between the original fork and v1.5.0, the repository developed an independent structured source model, governance metadata, deterministic generation, assurance reporting, standards-linked coverage, plain-English definitions, topic navigation, portfolio vocabulary, and its own publication and quality controls.

Version 2.0.0 recognizes that architectural reality and establishes an independent project.

## What independence changes

- ToIP is no longer the governing upstream.
- Repository drift is no longer treated as a synchronization obligation.
- External vocabularies enter through source intake and semantic review.
- TIG has its own concept identifiers, semantic model, release process, profiles, and generated representations.

## What independence does not change

- historical authorship and source attribution are retained;
- ToIP-derived concepts continue to identify their lineage;
- applicable license obligations remain in force;
- upstream terminology may continue to inform TIG through the same intake process as other source corpora.
