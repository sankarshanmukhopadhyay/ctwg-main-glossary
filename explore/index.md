## Search

The site search covers generated concept pages as well as governance and usage documentation. Alternative designations and aliases are shown on each concept page to help disambiguate terminology used across specifications.

## How to read a concept page

Each concept page presents information in layers:

1. **Plain-English meaning** for quick comprehension.
2. **Formal definition** for precise use.
3. **Why it matters** for implementation and governance context.
4. **Names and relationships** for navigating nearby concepts and other vocabularies.
5. **Provenance and identity** for stable reference and source inspection.
6. **Implementation and governance metadata** for assurance and machine-oriented consumers.
