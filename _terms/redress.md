# redress

A way for someone affected by a decision to challenge it and seek a correction or remedy.

## Formal definition
A governed path through which an affected party can challenge an outcome, seek correction or remedy, and obtain an accountable response.

## Why this concept matters
This is a cross-project glossary term. Individual specifications may define narrower domain-specific requirements.

Use this term to keep governance, authority, assurance, and implementation discussions consistent across interoperating projects.
